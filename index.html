What your front desk needs, without the clutter

Born in a real hotel that uses it every day. Every rule comes from something that actually went wrong.

No double bookings. Ever.

A database-level lock refuses two overlapping reservations in the same room, even if two people save in the same second.

Drag & drop calendar

Move a reservation to another room or other dates with the mouse. It always asks first, and explains why when a move is not possible.

The day at a glance

Arrivals, departures, in-house guests, free rooms and occupancy, one click away from each list.

Seasonal rates done right

A stay that crosses two seasons is priced night by night. No more prices typed by hand by mistake.

Housekeeping on a board

Every check-out creates the cleaning task: to clean, cleaning, inspection, clean.

Rooms out of order

Close a room for maintenance and it stops selling on the calendar and on every channel.

Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia and Agoda, both ways

  • Channel bookings, changes and cancellations come into Odoo by themselves (instantly with the webhook).
  • What you sell at the front desk, room closures and rates go out to Beds24 and every channel.
  • If a channel overbooks, the booking still comes in and you are warned: a real sale is never lost.
  • A send queue with retries: an internet outage never loses a change.
  • Connects in 5 minutes with a Beds24 invite code. Every switch starts off until you decide.
