One invoice for room, services and restaurant in Odoo

The most common question from hotels that move to Odoo: the guest ate at the restaurant, used the laundry and stayed three nights – how do I give them one invoice at check-out? Odoo has all the pieces (Point of Sale, Sales, Invoicing), but nothing that ties them to a room. This is the pattern that works.

The rule: everything goes to the folio, nothing is invoiced on its own

  • Each stay has a folio: the list of everything the guest consumed.
  • Room nights, extras (laundry, transfers, minibar) and restaurant tickets are lines on the folio.
  • At check-out you create one customer invoice from the folio, with Odoo’s own invoicing (taxes, fiscal positions and e-invoicing keep working).

Restaurant and bar: charge to the room from the POS

In the Point of Sale, the cashier pays the order with Odoo’s native Customer Account (pay later) method for the guest staying in the room. The ticket is then copied to that guest’s folio and marked so that neither the POS nor the folio invoices it twice. Two checks matter in practice: refuse the charge if the person is not checked in, and refuse it if the guest has more than one room (ask which one).

Partial invoices and changed stays

  • Invoice only the lines that have no invoice yet: you can bill the deposit now and the extras later.
  • If the stay gets longer, invoice only the new nights. If it gets shorter after invoicing, issue a credit note instead of editing a posted invoice.
  • The folio total with taxes should always equal the sum of its invoices: that is your control.
